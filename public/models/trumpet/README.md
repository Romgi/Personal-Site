# Trumpet model

[Trumpet](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/trumpet-1dc9efd37bf14d1b9d1e3de0ca90435c) by [Kagelok](https://sketchfab.com/Kagelok), licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) ([legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode)).

The original `scene.gltf` and `scene.bin` are preserved here. The glTF's embedded `asset.extras` records the author, original source and CC BY 4.0 license. These files were downloaded from the Munich Modern Jazz Orchestra's [public model directory](https://mmjo.de/assets/models/trumpet/scene.gltf); its [model credits](https://mmjo.de/3d-model-credits.html) also identify Kagelok and CC BY 4.0.

The website adapts the model at runtime by removing its presentation pose, converting to Y-up, centering and uniformly scaling it, and adjusting material roughness. Its topology, vertex normals, gold/silver color assignments and metalness are retained. Site lighting and camera movement are separate presentation changes.

The asset contains 28,076 triangles across 65 meshes, four materials and no textures. The glTF is 96,075 bytes and its binary buffer is 787,824 bytes.
